# AHW Architects — Services / Products

Status: **Approved working context**
Scope: **Global master brand**

AHW should present its capabilities as connected parts of one delivery system, while allowing clients to engage the specific service they need.

## 1. Architecture

Core capability:

- Architectural concept and development.
- Spatial planning.
- Design development.
- Technical coordination.
- Documentation appropriate to project scope.

Positioning:

Architecture should be presented as the foundation of the project's identity, performance, and buildability — not as an isolated visual exercise.

## 2. Interior Design

Core capability:

- Interior concept.
- Space planning.
- Material and finish strategy.
- Custom detailing.
- Joinery and interior coordination.
- Design development through delivery where contracted.

Positioning:

Interiors should balance character, usability, durability, detailing, and execution.

## 3. Engineering & Technical Coordination

Core capability:

- Engineering coordination within the approved project scope.
- Technical integration of architecture, interiors, building systems, and construction requirements.
- Design coordination intended to reduce site conflicts.

Do not imply that AHW provides every specialist engineering discipline on every project unless the project scope confirms it.

## 4. Design-Build

Core capability:

- Integrated design and construction delivery.
- Single-team coordination from design through execution where contracted.
- Procurement and construction interfaces.
- Quality and programme control.
- Handover.

Primary positioning:

> **One accountable team from concept to completion.**

This is the central commercial differentiator of the global brand.

## 5. Fit-Out

Core capability:

- Interior fit-out.
- Retail, commercial, hospitality, residential, and other applicable environments.
- Site execution.
- Finishes, joinery, specialist trades, and coordination within project scope.
- Quality control and handover.

Positioning:

Fit-out is treated as the physical realization of design intent, with emphasis on detailing, coordination, quality, and programme.

## 6. Renovation / Transformation

Where offered and supported by the project scope:

- Existing-space assessment.
- Replanning and redesign.
- Interior transformation.
- Upgrade and refurbishment.
- Construction delivery.

Do not promise structural alterations, MEP upgrades, authority approvals, or other specialist work unless the project scope confirms them.

## 7. How the Services Connect

The preferred narrative is not:

> "We sell six unrelated services."

It is:

> "We can enter at the point your project needs us, and when the scope calls for integration, the same delivery system can carry the project from design through build and handover."

## 8. Market Application

The global service architecture stays consistent.

Local pages may prioritize different services according to market demand and actual office capability. Local adaptation must not create contradictory claims about what AHW does globally.

## 9. Naming Discipline

Use the exact approved service name when linking to a site service page.

Do not create new service categories merely to make content sound more comprehensive.

## 10. Commercial Principle

The service description should always make clear what problem the capability solves:

- Architecture → vision, planning, identity, buildability.
- Interior Design → experience, function, materiality, detail.
- Engineering/coordination → technical integration and risk reduction.
- Design-Build → accountability and continuity.
- Fit-Out → controlled physical execution.
- Renovation/transformation → upgrading an existing asset with coordinated design and execution.
