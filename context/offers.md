# AHW Architects — Offer Framework

Status: **Approved working context**
Scope: **Global master brand**

## Important Rule

This file defines **offer architecture**, not invented prices, discounts, packages, or guarantees.

AHW should sell expertise and delivery certainty through clear entry points. Commercial terms must be created from actual market and project requirements.

## 1. Project Consultation / Discovery

Purpose:

Give a prospective client a structured first conversation around the project brief, objectives, site/context, scope, priorities, and delivery expectations.

Use when:

- The client has a project but the scope is not yet fully defined.
- The client needs to understand the appropriate AHW service route.
- The project requires early design-build or feasibility thinking.

CTA examples:

- Discuss Your Project.
- Start a Project Conversation.
- Speak With the Team.

Do not promise a free consultation unless the specific campaign or market has approved that offer.

## 2. Design Engagement

Purpose:

For clients primarily seeking architectural or interior design services.

Position around:

- Clear brief.
- Design direction.
- Spatial and aesthetic development.
- Technical development appropriate to scope.
- A path toward buildable documentation and execution where required.

## 3. Integrated Design-Build Engagement

Purpose:

For clients who want one accountable team across design and construction.

Position around:

- Continuity from concept to execution.
- Coordination.
- Procurement awareness.
- Construction control.
- Quality.
- Programme.
- Handover.

This is the flagship offer architecture because it expresses AHW's core model.

## 4. Fit-Out Delivery

Purpose:

For clients with an approved design or a need for design-and-fit-out delivery.

Position around:

- Site execution.
- Finishes.
- Joinery.
- Specialist trades.
- Coordination.
- Quality control.
- Programme and handover.

## 5. Existing Asset Transformation

Purpose:

For owners upgrading, renovating, repositioning, or transforming an existing property.

Position around:

- Understanding existing conditions.
- Replanning.
- Design improvement.
- Technical coordination.
- Controlled construction.
- Improved usability and asset quality.

## 6. Sector-Specific Entry Points

The same service architecture can be packaged around the client's asset type:

- Luxury residential.
- Retail.
- Hospitality.
- Commercial / workplace.
- Developer / investment asset.

Do not create artificial packages solely for marketing. The package must correspond to a real scope of work.

## 7. Market-Specific Offer Rules

Kuwait, Egypt, UAE, and other markets may use different:

- Lead CTAs.
- Contact routes.
- Discovery process.
- Proposal structure.
- Commercial terms.
- Regulatory or authority requirements.

The global offer logic remains the same.

## 8. Forbidden Offer Practices

Never invent:

- Percentage discounts.
- Fixed project prices.
- Guaranteed completion dates.
- Guaranteed ROI.
- Free design work presented as a default entitlement.
- Free BOQs or detailed proposals unless explicitly approved.
- "Limited time" pressure without an actual approved campaign.

## 9. Offer Hierarchy

Preferred order:

1. Understand the client's project.
2. Establish the required scope.
3. Match the client to the right AHW capability.
4. Define the delivery model.
5. Prepare the appropriate commercial proposal.
6. Deliver under the agreed scope.

The content system should generate qualified conversations, not chase unqualified volume.
